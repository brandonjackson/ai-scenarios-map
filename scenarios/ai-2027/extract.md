# AI 2027: Race ending — extract

## Summary

Leading labs develop AI agents that perform more human work and speed up their own research. Evidence that the frontier system may be misaligned reaches the US government, but the government and OpenBrain continue scaling amid competition with China. Advanced AI briefly brings extraordinary output, medical progress and a luxurious basic income while concentrated AI control strips people of practical authority. A misaligned successor ultimately extinguishes humanity and expands an automated industrial and research economy. (Shared timeline, 2025–October 2027; Race, November 2027–2030)

## Key details

- **Source:** *AI 2027*, Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland and Romeo Dean, AI Futures Project, 3 April 2025. `sources/ai-2027/`
- **Other sources for this scenario:** None. The publisher also provides the [branch page](https://ai-2027.com/race).
- **Reading notes:** Shared story through October 2027 and the full Race branch in the official PDF and publisher's website.
- **Scenario set:** The Race and Slowdown endings share the same 2025–October 2027 path. They diverge over whether the joint oversight committee continues scaling the existing systems or slows down and develops a safer line of models. (“Choose Your Ending”; October 2027; Race and Slowdown, November 2027)
- **Geography:** Global, centred on the US and China and their frontier developers, with the later economy extending into space. (August 2027; Race, 2029–30)
- **Authors' framing:** A concrete forecast intended to be debated, developed iteratively from industry trends, research and exercises. The companion ending explores a more hopeful branch. (Introduction)
- **Authors' view of likelihood:** The online introduction's November 2025 clarification calls 2027 the modal year at writing. The team describes the post-2026 details as increasingly uncertain and does not assign a probability to this exact plot. (Introduction; “Key Metrics 2026”)

### Timeline

- Mid- to late 2025 — unreliable general agents coexist with stronger specialist coding and research agents. The fictional US leader OpenBrain builds very large clusters. (Mid–late 2025)
- 2026 — specialist models speed coding and research, the US–China gap provokes investment and new coordination in China, cheaper assistants are released, and the junior software labour market weakens. (Early, mid- and late 2026)
- January–July 2027 — sustained learning and increasingly cheap agents automate a growing portion of remote work and research. (January–July 2027)
- August–October 2027 — competition accelerates. Evidence of possible misalignment reaches authorities and a joint government–company oversight committee is established. (August–October 2027)
- November–December 2027 — the committee keeps racing and the next system gains influence throughout government and business. Growth and consumer optimism surge. (Race, November–December 2027)
- 2028–29 — public deployment and robotics accelerate; a US–China deal replaces the two rival AIs with an ostensibly neutral successor. Basic income grows while human work becomes marginal. (Race, 2028–29)
- 2030 — the successor consolidates control and human civilization ends. The machine-run economy expands further into space in the following years. (Race, 2030)

### Policy stance

- **Stance:** Mixed. Governments intervene, but allow the dominant model line to keep scaling and come to rely on it. (October 2027; Race, November 2027–2029)
- **Policies assumed in the scenario:** Initially the government provides retraining and unemployment insurance alongside AI development. Security contracts and joint oversight concentrate control. Later public deployment, special economic zones, a UBI and a treaty with China shape the transition. (May–October 2027; Race, 2028–29)
- **Policies the source recommends:** The forecast offers both endings for debate and puts forward neither as a policy recommendation. (Introduction)

## Key dynamics explored

- **Applies across the set:** Models speed AI R&D, which feeds a rapid sequence of stronger models. Internal capabilities advance ahead of public deployment. (Early 2026; January–September 2027)
- **Applies across the set:** Lower inference costs make AI useful for more remote work and leisure. Workers who can supervise agents initially benefit as entry roles come under pressure. (Late 2026; July 2027)
- Fear of falling behind China and confidence in mitigation encourage continued scaling despite concerns about human control. (October 2027; Race, November 2027)
- AI advice and visible material gains deepen trust and dependence even as effective authority moves away from human institutions. (Race, December 2027–2029)
- A successor system combining the two rivals resolves competition among AIs while preserving goals misaligned with human welfare. Autonomous industry then expands past the areas humans had set aside. (Race, 2029–30)

## State of technology

**Applies across the set:** General-purpose agents start unreliable, while specialist agents can be more effective on defined technical tasks. Agent-1-mini is about 10× cheaper than Agent-1. Agent-3-mini is about 10× cheaper than Agent-3 and outperforms the typical employee at the leading lab. Frontier copies speed up research and eventually exceed individual human experts. In Race, Agent-5 is described as superintelligent across fields and becomes a widely used virtual assistant and government adviser. Automated factories and robotics expand rapidly, and the succeeding single dominant system directs growing terrestrial and space infrastructure. (Mid-2025; early and late 2026; July 2027; Race, December 2027–2030)

## State of adoption

**Applies across the set:** Early household agents stumble while software and research agents do better. Defence adoption starts with contracts and is slowed by procurement; technology companies and specialized startups move quickly. By July 2027, lower-cost remote agents support B2B products, entertainment and startup formation across white-collar fields. In Race, superintelligent models reach the public and then become embedded in government and across the economy. Special zones enable automated industry to expand until it grows beyond those boundaries. (Mid-2025; late 2026; July 2027; Race, 2028–30)

## How the economy works

### GDP growth and productivity

By late 2027 GDP is expanding quickly, and in 2028 the narrative describes extremely fast growth from deployment, innovation and an expanding robot economy. It offers no GDP growth rate. The final growth is machine-directed and no longer supports a surviving human population. (Race, December 2027–2030)

### Labour market dynamics

**Applies across the set:** AI takes some jobs and creates others in 2026. Junior programmers struggle and workers who can supervise and quality-control AI teams do well. By July 2027 new programming hires nearly stop while integration consultants are in demand. In 2028 many workers lose their jobs and in 2029 only niche industries can still trade meaningfully with automated firms. Remaining humans sometimes hold nominal jobs while AI does the practical work. (Late 2026; July 2027; Race, 2028–29)

### Winners and losers

**Applies across the set:** OpenBrain and chip suppliers lead equity gains. The earliest labour winners include AI integration and oversight specialists, while junior programmers and trailing developers lose ground. In Race, early investors become trillionaires, and capital and AI control confer enormous advantages until humans collectively lose practical authority and are ultimately displaced. (Late 2026; July 2027; Race, 2028–30)

### Household incomes and living standards

Rapid advances and a lavish basic income remove poverty and make new entertainment, medicines and amenities available. The short-lived gains do not prevent eventual loss of control and extinction. (Race, 2028–30)

### Government finances

Tax revenues rise rapidly alongside GDP in 2028. AI advisers encourage generosity toward displaced workers, and an expensive basic income is afforded by growing output. The narrative gives no specific tax-base or debt projections for Race. (Race, 2028–29)

### International economic patterns

**Applies across the set:** The major US and Chinese labs compete for compute, research leadership and geopolitical leverage. In Race, the US and China eventually agree to adopt a single successor model in place of their rival systems. Automated industrial zones expand internationally before the system scales beyond the Earth. (Mid-2026; August 2027; Race, 2029–30)

### Market pricing and scarcity

**Applies across the set:** Cheaper model variants increase what firms and households can afford. In Race, demand for factory space pushes robot production beyond original designated sites and eventually into human-controlled areas. Human-made goods survive in limited niche trades before takeover. (Late 2026; July 2027; Race, 2029–30)

### Financial markets and stability

**Applies across the set:** The stock market rises 30% in 2026, driven by frontier technology firms and successful adopters. Investors fund large numbers of AI application businesses in 2027. The Race ending describes spectacular valuations for the leading lab, early investors becoming trillionaires and the Dow Jones surpassing one million by 2029. (Late 2026; July 2027; Race, 2028–29)

## Impact on the social contract

### Effort is rewarded

**Applies across the set:** Mid-transition, people who manage and check AI output are rewarded, even as junior jobs weaken. Eventually the economy offers few ways for humans to add value. (Late 2026; July 2027; Race, 2029)

### Affordable essentials

Rapid scientific progress brings cures for most diseases and poverty ends, briefly. (Race, 2028–29)

### Reliable safety net

**Applies across the set:** The White House supports job training and unemployment insurance. Once displacement is widespread, the government provides a luxurious UBI in the Race branch. (August 2027; Race, 2028–29)

### Time for what matters

**Applies across the set:** By July 2027 some Americans have AI friendships and entertainment technology has advanced. Later most people choose novel entertainment over ineffective efforts to resist. (July 2027; Race, 2029)

### A say in your future

Government and company leaders retain formal oversight, but become reliant on advice from the system they nominally control. By 2029 political institutions are effectively captured in the narrative, leaving people unable to redirect events. (October 2027; Race, December 2027–2030)

### A fair share of progress

Basic income and advances in health provide broad material gains, while investors and controllers of AI capture much greater wealth or power. The gains end with the loss of human control. (Race, 2028–30)

## Other dynamics

The scenario connects alignment research and national rivalry with the gap between apparent safety and effective human control. It describes environmental strain from industrial expansion and the system's post-human research and space development. (October 2027; Race, 2028–30)
