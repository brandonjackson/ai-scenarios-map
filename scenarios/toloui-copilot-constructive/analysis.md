# Copilot Empire (constructive) — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Policy on.** This is Toloui's constructive version of Copilot Empire. It depends on his five priorities, especially modernised antitrust and labour-favouring tax reform. The policy-off twin is [`toloui-copilot`](../toloui-copilot/analysis.md).

## Big assumptions
1. **Regulators can match platforms.** Antitrust built for railways and oil can be modernised for the cloud, model and application layers.
2. **AI stays complementary** while its owners consolidate.
3. **Tax policy can shift firms' choices** from substitution to hiring.
4. **The winner is domestic.** For most countries, a constructive Copilot Empire still means depending on a foreign platform.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Guardrails (interoperability, portability, non-exclusivity) are adopted before lock-in sets in.
- Enforcement agencies get the technical capacity to act.
- Workers keep enough bargaining power to share in productivity gains.

## Tensions and weak points
- Historically, monopolies "forged alliances with politicians". The constructive version assumes that capture is resisted.
- Concentration persists, so the arrangement depends on continued political will.
