# Copilot Empire (constructive) — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**On.** The constructive form is defined by intervention: an advance roadmap of competition guardrails and escalation triggers across cloud, compute, model and application layers. The extract treats the other priorities (tax, procurement, training, safety) as measures that can promote augmentation, without a fixed package, so only the competition regime is counted as assumed. The policy-off twin is [`toloui-copilot`](../toloui-copilot/analysis.md).

## Big assumptions
1. **Capability concentrates but stays complementary.** As in Copilot Empire, high fixed costs favour a few providers while AI augments workers.
2. **Adoption can be steered.** Procurement metrics (output per hour, time to proficiency, customer satisfaction), training partnerships and public R&D encourage in-house worker capability.
3. **Markets stay contestable at the edges.** Interoperability and portability make switching viable without forcing break-ups.
4. **Politics keeps up.** Enforcement agencies gain the technical capacity to act, and the triggers are pulled when needed.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Guardrails are adopted before lock-in sets in.
- DOJ and FTC build AI and cloud expertise.
- Providers cannot capture the regulators who set the triggers.
- AI stays a complement rather than reaching broad absolute advantage.

## Tensions and weak points
- The catalogue description says labour-tax reform keeps the platforms in check; the new extract treats tax reform as an optional supporting measure, and the competition regime as the defining policy. Links now cover only the competition rules.
- The polarisation precedent (rising high-skill and stagnant lower-skill wages) still applies, and the competition rules do not address it directly.
- The arrangement depends on continued political will while concentration persists; historical monopolists cultivated political allies.
- Close neighbours: `tbi`, `tcb-augmentation` and `gos-augmented-growth`; it sits between Copilot Empire and Promethean Fire, as the framework places it.
