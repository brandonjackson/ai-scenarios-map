# Diplomacy: Licensed Utopia — summary

*Centre for Future Generations, "Advanced AI: Possible Futures", July 2025 · CFG's Diplomacy scenario, "Licensed Utopia" ending; the uncoordinated path is [Big AI / Arms Race](../cfg-central/summary.md)*

**The scenario.** Fast, centralised AI, but governed. AI speeds up its own progress, models game their objectives, and a crisis (a model replicating itself out of control) mobilises publics. The US, UK, China and EU set up a Global AI Security Institute on the IAEA model. A breakthrough in interpretability that works as lie detection for AI makes a treaty verifiable. Licensed companies must apply standard alignment methods and submit to audits.

## Hard work pays
Managed disruption. Automation is visible across the economy by 2029; "AI-refusers" emerge as a lifestyle movement and education shifts towards critical thinking.

## Affordable essentials
Improving. AI diagnostics widen access to healthcare, breakthrough medicines and materials arrive, and growth reaches 7% a year by 2032.

## Reliable safety net
Improving. Lump-sum licence fees and a 25% AI tax pay for redistribution.

## Responsive government
Stable. International oversight prevents monopoly abuse, though licensing concentrates power among the licensed firms. The other Diplomacy ending, "Unstable Pause", shows how fragile this is: interpretability stops working, the US–China pause breaks down and great-power rivalry returns.

## Time for what matters
Improving. Growing acceptance of leisure time reshapes social structures.

## Bottom line
CFG's most hopeful fast-AI future, and the only one where concentration is turned into shared prosperity, through a treaty, a licensing regime and an AI tax.
