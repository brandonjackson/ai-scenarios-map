# d/acc: My Techno-Optimism — summary

*Vitalik Buterin, 27 November 2023*

**The scenario.** A reply to both "effective accelerationism" and AI pessimism. Technology is "amazing" and delaying it has "very high costs" (Buterin points to life expectancy), but the *direction* of technology matters as much as its speed. **d/acc** means defensive, decentralised, democratic, differential acceleration: deliberately building technologies that favour defence over offence and spread power rather than concentrate it, across physical defence, bio (far-UVC, cheap tests, better masks), cyber (cryptography, blockchains), information (community notes, prediction markets) and social technology. Superintelligent AI is the "elephant in the room". Buterin takes existential risk seriously and fears that even a "friendly" AI-run world leaves humans as "pets".

## Hard work pays
Implicitly protected, through augmentation. The "happy path" is to "merge with the AIs": tools that enhance human cognition instead of replacing it, from AI drawing tools that keep humans involved throughout to brain–computer interfaces. Buterin fears a Culture-style world where "almost anything [humans] do, a machine could do better."

## Affordable essentials
Rising, as a by-product of technological progress, which he celebrates (health, longevity, cheaper goods). He says outcomes like environmental gains came from "coordinated intention" rather than automatic market forces.

## Reliable safety net
Not directly addressed. The essay focuses on safety from catastrophe and tyranny, not income security. A safer, more defence-favouring world reduces the losses from conflict, crime and pandemics.

## Responsive government
The essay's strongest concern. With AI, "the sky is near, the emperor is everywhere": the attention and distance limits that once constrained despots disappear. The same managerial technology that lets 500 OpenAI staff serve 100 million users could let "a 500-person political elite, or even a 5-person board" rule a country. Robot armies remove the "human in the loop" check on war. Defence-favouring technology (like Switzerland's mountains) helps "healthier, more open and more freedom-respecting forms of governance" survive. Buterin distrusts both one global AI body and an unstable "polytheistic" multipolar AI world. In his polls, majorities preferred a decade's delay to AI monopolised by any single group.

## Time for what matters
Valued but not developed. The objection to the Culture is about meaning: humans "need to be needed".

## Bottom line
A prescription more than a forecast: steer technology toward a world where people stay in charge and power stays distributed. The economic promises are mostly left implicit.
