# d/acc: My Techno-Optimism — summary

*Vitalik Buterin, "My techno-optimism", 27 November 2023. A prescription for steering technology plus thought experiments about possible futures; global, with examples from Switzerland and the Ethereum ecosystem. No milestones or probabilities of its own. Read from the full text; see `extract.md`.*

**The scenario.** Defensive and decentralising technologies (public-health resilience, cyber security, information integrity, tools for deliberation and privacy-preserving identity) improve people's ability to protect themselves, verify information and coordinate without depending on a few powerful actors. AI development favours tools that extend human cognition and keep people in control, with caution about autonomous superintelligence. The result is continued progress with more resilience and more room for democratic government.

## Hard work pays
Not addressed directly. The labour section says AI can complement human cognition and widen what workers can do, but gives no figures for jobs or wages. The essay warns that sophisticated augmentation could at first amplify the advantages of already powerful groups.

## Affordable essentials
Resilience rather than cheapness. Public-health resilience, safer environments and more self-sufficient access to necessities help communities withstand crises, and resilient local systems reduce dependence on fragile long supply chains.

## Reliable safety net
Not addressed.

## Responsive government
The essay's main concern. Stronger defence creates room for autonomy and democracy; without it, citizens are pushed into reliance on central platforms or authorities. AI weakens old limits on central rule and surveillance. A single authority controlling advanced AI would hold exceptional power, while competition among providers may drift towards concentration. Privacy-preserving identity, collective evidence tools and better deliberation help communities coordinate.

## Time for what matters
Framed as purpose. The essay doubts that leisure and comfort in an AI-run society are enough when machines do everything better; a better future keeps choices and projects where people have a real effect.

## Bottom line
A prescription for keeping power spread out and people in charge. Its economic promises are mostly implicit.
