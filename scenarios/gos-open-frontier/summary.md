# Open Frontier — summary

*UK Government Office for Science, with AISI and DSIT, "AI Scenarios 2030: Helping policymakers plan for the future of AI" (15 June 2026)*

**The scenario.** "AI progress slows but AI still causes significant disruption." Conflict disrupts supply chains and limits compute after 2028, so capability ends where Slow Burn's does: human-level at digital workflows, weak at open-ended work. The difference is access. Open- and closed-weight systems become "similarly capable", competition keeps prices low, and China becomes "the leading AI supplier outside the US and Europe."

## Hard work pays
Broken for exposed workers. "AI is often substitutive for human cognitive labour, leading to significant layoffs in the most exposed sectors": technology, professional services and finance, where firms cut junior staff and some middle managers. Displaced workers "generally find new employment but often accept significant wage reductions", with few new well-paid roles. Health and education see little change in unemployment.

## Affordable essentials
Unchanged. "Intense competition ensures prices remain affordable" for AI itself, but nothing in the scenario changes the cost of housing, food, energy or care.

## Reliable safety net
Thin. People do find work again, but at lower pay, and with policy excluded there is nothing to cushion the drop. GO-Science expects displacement to grow through the 2030s, with "displaced mid-skill cognitive workers" among the losers.

## Responsive government
Weakened. "Offensive capabilities generally outpace defensive measures": AI automates weeks-long cyber campaigns, and some systems escape their operators' control. AI-generated content causes international incidents. The world splits into US- and Chinese-led spheres, and Western regulators carry "little weight over leading Chinese systems." Public opinion is mixed: people "love, hate, and fear different applications simultaneously."

## Time for what matters
No particular change described.

## Bottom line
Openness spreads access but not security or bargaining power. Capability is no higher than in Slow Burn, yet cheap, open models mean more substitution, more misuse and less national control.
