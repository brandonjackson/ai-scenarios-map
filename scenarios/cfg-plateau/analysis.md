# Plateau — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Mostly policy off; technology-driven.** The good outcome comes from a *technical* ceiling (data walls, failed scaling), not from regulation. Policy appears only in the Mayhem ending, as a reactive ban on large open models after misuse. Nothing in the Bright Winter ending is attributed to deliberate policy.

## Big assumptions
1. **Scaling stalls soon.** Data is exhausted, synthetic data "proves ineffective", agents stay unreliable, and evaluation is easily gamed, all by around 2027–29. This bets against the industry's own forecasts and against continued algorithmic progress.
2. **Capital retreats in an orderly way.** Mega-project funding "dries up" without a disorderly crash. A bursting AI investment bubble could itself be a large macroeconomic shock (compare Doctorow and Citrini).
3. **Small models are good enough** for most uses, so decentralisation follows naturally from the plateau.
4. **Gradual adoption means gradual labour impact.** The scenario assumes that slower change gives "time to adapt" and that existing skills and institutions are enough.
5. **Geopolitics calms down.** Once AI isn't seen as militarily decisive, the US–China race loses its edge.

## What must be true for this to occur
- No new paradigm (new architectures, reasoning methods, embodied data) gets past the data wall within the period.
- Open-weight models reach "good enough" quality and stay cheap enough to run locally.
- Investors accept lower returns without a financial crisis.
- For Bright Winter rather than Mayhem: defensive cyber capabilities improve as fast as offensive misuse of open models.

## Tensions and weak points
- Even a "plateaued" AI can be disruptive at the entry level. CFG's account of office workers adopting tools gradually doesn't address junior hiring (see Windfall's Lost Generation).
- The two endings show that decentralisation is ambivalent: the same openness that spreads benefits also spreads harm, and the policy response (bans) re-centralises.
- **Map labelling note:** CFG's published report has five scenarios, *Plateau*, *Big AI*, *Diplomacy* and *Arms Race* plus endings. The map's "Centralized Takeoff" and "Decentralized Takeoff" aren't CFG's own names (see those folders).
