# Plateau — summary

*Centre for Future Generations (Özcan, Juijn, Graabak & Bogerd), "Advanced AI: Possible Futures", July 2025 · gradual progress, decentralised development*

**The scenario.** AI hits hard technical limits between 2025 and 2029. High-quality training data runs out, synthetic data disappoints, agents struggle with long multi-step tasks, and scaling gives diminishing returns. Funding for mega-projects dries up. The result is useful but narrow AI: smaller, cheaper models that run locally, a thriving open-source ecosystem, and incremental adoption. CFG gives two endings: **"A Bright Winter"**, where the scaling bet fails and society adapts calmly, and **"Decentralised Mayhem"**, where a capable model's guardrails are breached in late 2027, uncensored variants spread, and automated hacking and disinformation lead to harmonised bans on large open models.

## Hard work pays
Kept. Office workers adopt AI tools gradually rather than being displaced, and "the slower pace of change gives most people time to adapt." Cheap local models raise the productivity of individual workers and small firms instead of replacing them.

## Affordable essentials
Stable. Gains are modest and concentrated in coding and specialist fields. Energy worries persist but efficiency improves. Nothing in the scenario moves the cost of housing, food or care much either way.

## Reliable safety net
Stable. Without mass displacement the existing net copes. In the Mayhem ending, rising cybercrime and ransomware add costs and insecurity rather than unemployment.

## Responsive government
Mixed by ending. Power stays dispersed: the US–China arms-race story fades and Europe gets a chance to catch up. In Bright Winter, defences improve alongside rising cybercrime. In Decentralised Mayhem, trust erodes and governments end up in "a new digital equilibrium defined by constant vigilance rather than trust", reacting after the fact instead of steering.

## Time for what matters
Little change. Personal coding assistants, research tools and digital companions make everyday life easier at the edges. CFG doesn't project shifts in working hours or community life.

## Bottom line
The calmest future in the CFG set: AI as a widely spread, modest tool with the social contract mostly intact. The open ecosystem behind its good ending also carries its main risk, which is misuse.
