# Supercompetent AI — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Off.** The model has no government, and competitive markets produce "abundance without concentration" on their own. The authors' only recommendations are about measurement.

## Big assumptions
1. **Capability:** AI beats every human in every productive domain (task skills 12 against a best human of 9 in the example), but its coordination stays at or below the threshold (≈ 0.78, the human average).
2. **Adoption:** cost-minimising firms adopt freely, and copies are rented at compute cost.
3. **Markets:** firm size is set by coordination alone, so better competence lowers costs without making firms bigger. Free entry keeps industries competitive across many lean firms.
4. **Politics:** absent.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Competence keeps advancing much faster than coordination. The authors say current progress runs mostly along the competence axis.
- AI becomes better than the best human at every task, including physical tasks if robotics counts as a domain, while multi-agent systems still hit diminishing returns.
- Humans keep enough of a coordination advantage over AI to earn a premium directing AI teams.

## Tensions and weak points
- **"Headcounts likely shrink" against full employment.** The essay expects shrinking headcounts, and the Figure 22 path has productive workers leaving employment. The paper's example keeps all 14 workers employed. The scores follow the example, and the essay's version would score lower on the safety net.
- **Coordinators as winners.** Coordinators are called relative winners, but in the example their pay still falls from 1.30 to 0.48. They win only against specialists.
- **Abundance without a distribution story.** Prices fall about sevenfold from minimal AGI, but capital, ownership and household incomes are not addressed. The diffuse x_labor rests on competitive markets and consumer gains, not on incomes.
- **Neighbours.** Above `anthropic-substantial` on replacement but more diffuse, because markets stay competitive. In the set it sits between minimal AGI and the two supercooperative regimes.

## Scoring notes
Scores are 2/–/3*/–/–. Work is 2: everyone in the example still has a job, but specialist pay falls to 0.39 and coordinator pay to 0.48, close to the 0.25 outside option. Coordinators keep a premium, so the harm is severe rather than universal. Safety net is 3, inferred: there are no transfers, but displacement in the example moves specialists to niche markets, not into unemployment, so the net is not stretched. The essay's shrinking headcounts are the main risk to this score. Essentials, government and time are not addressed. With two promises scored, there is no headline rating.
