# Supercompetent AI — summary

*"Organizing Intelligence", Alex Imas, Andrew Koh and Shane Legg (Google DeepMind), arXiv:2611.05417, 31 October 2026, with the companion DeepMind Institute essay "The Economics of Intelligence" (29 October 2026). A theoretical model, explicitly not a forecast, that sorts AGI into four regimes by task competence and coordination. This is the regime where AI beats every human expert at every task but still coordinates no better than the average person. Read from the paper's main sections and Appendix E and from the essay in full; see `extract.md`.*

**The scenario.** "Surprisingly, there is still little structural transformation." Coordination still caps how big an AI team can usefully get (about seven copies), so firms "become far more productive without getting larger; in fact, headcounts and scale likely shrink." Industries stay competitive across many lean firms. Goods and services become "cheap and abundant", with deep-market prices falling from 0.202 to about 0.028. The authors call it "abundance without concentration". Inside firms, roles flip: AI does standalone specialist work at the cost of compute, specialists retreat to niche markets, and human coordinators who direct AI teams become the scarce skill.

## Hard work pays
Broken for some. Everyone in the worked example still has a job, but wages fall close to the outside option of 0.25: specialists to 0.39 (from 2.90 under minimal AGI and 4.15 before AI) and coordinators to 0.48. Pay for "routine specialist tasks gradually declines". Coordinators keep a premium because "someone who sets direction and keeps the whole effort aligned multiplies the output of a team of supercompetent AI agents". People "do not vanish from production; their comparative advantage shifts toward judgment, orchestration".

## Affordable essentials
Not addressed. Goods and services grow "cheap and abundant", but the source does not discuss housing, food, energy or care.

## Reliable safety net
Under little strain in the model. It has no taxes or transfers, but the example keeps everyone employed. Workers are displaced across markets, not into unemployment, although the essay expects headcounts to shrink.

## Responsive government
Not addressed. The model has no government.

## Time for what matters
Not addressed.

## Bottom line
Large gains with no reorganisation: lean, competitive firms make cheap goods while human pay falls toward the outside option.
