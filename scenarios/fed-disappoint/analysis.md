# SI Disappoints — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Off.** The scenario describes a market correction, slowdown and recovery with no government or central-bank response written in. Warsh recommends no policies.

## Big assumptions
1. **Capability.** SI does not deliver the productivity gains investors expected. The speech does not say how it falls short.
2. **Adoption.** Heavy capital investment comes first; the returns then disappoint.
3. **Markets.** Expected returns turn down, equity valuations fall, and the fall leads to a slowdown with "a more notable rise in unemployment".
4. **Politics.** None stated.

## What must be true for this to occur
- AI investment and stock prices run well ahead of what the technology delivers.
- Expected returns are revised down sharply enough to cut equity valuations.
- The economy recovers on its own, back to the old trend, "without the productivity gains".

## Tensions and weak points
- Its unemployment is cyclical, not automation-driven, so it says little about augmentation or replacement; with no winners or losers named, the labour-gains pair is left blank.
- Public finances are inferred as weaker from the slowdown and rising unemployment; good jobs are fewer for a while but recover, so both fiscal-pair values are mild.
- The speech calls the unemployment rise "more notable" than in the displacement scenario but gives no size or length.
- Close to `dsit-steady` (AI disappoints) and to the 2027 correction in `imf-baseline`; as of late 2026 the investment boom it starts from is still running.

## Scoring notes
Scores are 2/–/3*/–/–, so only two promises are scored, below the three needed for a headline rating. Work is 2: the slowdown brings a more notable rise in unemployment than the displacement scenario, and the recovery comes without the productivity gains that would have raised pay. Safety net is 3, inferred: the job losses come from a slowdown that ends in recovery, so they are temporary rather than permanent and stay within what existing support handles. Essentials, government and time are not addressed.
