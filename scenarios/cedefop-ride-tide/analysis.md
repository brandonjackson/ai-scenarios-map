# Left Alone to Ride the Tide — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Off.** The defining feature is retreat: the state and employers step back from skills development, neither prioritises inclusion, regulation fails to address quality, privacy and ethics, and sustainability policy weakens "for fear of undermining competitiveness". The authors' call to build "capability alongside increasing responsibility" is a recommendation, not part of the scenario.

## Big assumptions
1. **Fast, broad capability.** AI arrives in successive waves and transforms most jobs; it augments slightly more often than it replaces.
2. **Cost-driven adoption.** Firms pick augmentation or replacement on cost, and integration varies dramatically across organisations and sectors.
3. **Tight labour markets.** The workforce shrinks or stagnates, so those who fall behind end up in worse jobs rather than unemployed.
4. **Institutional retreat.** The state, employers and unions all withdraw from skills development and worker support.

## What must be true for this to occur
- AI disruption moves faster than firms and workers can comfortably manage.
- Employers invest only in top talent and treat the broader workforce as self-financing.
- Non-standard work overtakes full-time, long-term employment (51% of respondents expected it to fall below 40%).
- Public skills policy shrinks rather than expands in response to disruption.

## Tensions and weak points
- The losers are "smaller in number than those who benefit", yet the scenario is framed around deepening inequality. On the map this puts it in the concentrated half but not at the extreme.
- Tight labour markets and a stagnating workforce sit oddly beside widespread precarity: scarcity should give workers leverage, but here it does so only for top talent.
- Wages, incomes and government finances are not addressed, so the fiscal pair is blank. Job quality is clearly worse, but there is no basis for saying what happens to public budgets.
- Respondents had their lowest confidence (2.97/4) in how AI-transformed learning unfolds, the very area the scenario is about.
- Neighbours are `acemoglu`, `wef-stalled`, `tcb-concentrated` and `chiang`: polarised gains without mass replacement. It is the most negative of those on job security and voice.
