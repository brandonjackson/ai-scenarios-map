# AI Unleashed — summary

*Cedefop (European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training), "Preparing for 2040: four AI-powered scenarios for the future of continuing skills development", Publications Office of the European Union (November 2025). Four exploratory EU scenarios built from 21 STEEPV trends, morphological analysis and a semi-Delphi survey of 132 respondents; not a 2×2 grid, and no probabilities. This is Scenario D, the "dystopian future" of the set, built on the path where AI "replaces almost all jobs and serves only the few controlling it". Read from the report's full text; see `extract.md`. After the survey, the authors dropped the initial claim that AI fully replaces teachers and formal education.*

**The scenario.** It is 2040. AI and automation have "taken over in all areas of life and work", with "unprecedented job losses across all sectors, industries and skills levels". "A few major players own and control AI technologies and have emerged as the dominant force in the global economy", and their influence over dependent policymakers leads to "the rise of authoritarian powers at the service of (or controlled by) the few". No regulation safeguards citizens' rights. Remaining workers do non-standard work on tasks AI cannot yet do, such as domestic service or training machines. Environmental policy loses ground, and AI drives geographical polarisation, brain drain and rising irregular migration.

## Hard work pays
Broken for all. "Most human jobs are destroyed and replaced by AI"; recruitment "is limited to highly AI-skilled individuals", and companies see little point in developing "a disposable human labour force". Remaining jobs are "often precarious, low-paying and lacking in benefits", and careers are fragmented by "frequent and involuntary career changes".

## Affordable essentials
Not addressed. Poverty spreads, but the cost of essentials is not discussed.

## Reliable safety net
Broken for all. "Large segments of the population are completely pushed out of the labour market and increasingly experience unemployment, poverty and social exclusion." Job losses are "not counterbalanced by measures safeguarding workers' rights"; "individuals are left alone and unsupported", and unions "no longer have the power" to protect workers. Only 11% of respondents expected AI's benefits to be equitably redistributed.

## Responsive government
Broken for all. "Democracy is increasingly undermined and extremist movements, fuelled by anger and frustration, begin to emerge." Those controlling AI "exert their full dominance over public institutions and decision-makers", algorithmic manipulation creates "filter bubbles", and citizens are "left disempowered", though some bottom-up anti-technology movements arise.

## Time for what matters
Broken for some. People "prioritise their own survival", purpose-driven careers stall and AI tools bring "increased isolation, anxiety and health issues". Some respondents saw pervasive job loss leading people towards volunteering and time with loved ones, and 25% expected fewer hours rather than dismissals.

## Bottom line
The set's warning: unchecked AI concentrates power and wealth, empties the labour market and hollows out democracy.
