# The Hollow Economy — summary

*Christian Catalini, Xiang Hui and Jane Wu, "Some Simple Economics of AGI", arXiv 2602.20946 (February 2026). The paper models AI as driving the cost of automating measurable tasks towards the cost of compute, while the human capacity to verify what agents produce stays bounded by time and experience. It ends in two equilibrium "basins": this one, the default drift if verification is left unmanaged, and the Augmented Economy (`catalini-augmented`). The authors frame it as a warning. No horizon year is given. Read from most of the full text, with the formal proofs skimmed; see `extract.md`.*

**The scenario.** A "Measurability Gap" opens between what agents can do and what people can afford to check. Deployers capture the gains of unverified deployment while the risks are socialised. Firms stop hiring juniors, so the next generation of verifiers never trains (the "Missing Junior Loop"), and experts' reviews become the training data that replaces them (the "Codifier's Curse"). Firms fall back on "AI verifying AI", which breeds false confidence. Unverified output that hits metrics but misses intent, "counterfeit utility", piles up as hidden debt. Measured GDP "explodes" while the surplus left for people shrinks, until an unexpected event triggers "a sudden systemic shock".

## Hard work pays
Broken for all. Wages for any cheaply measurable task fall towards the cost of compute "regardless of the historical prestige or training required". Entry-level hiring freezes, the middle tier of managers is hollowed out, "the value of average expertise collapses", and experts who check AI's work are training their own replacements. For displaced workers the paper's advice is "exit". The labour market splits into "an elite of high-leverage 'centaurs' and a vast, unaugmented underclass".

## Affordable essentials
Squeezed. The paper does not discuss housing, food or care. But resources are "siphoned to fuel the agentic scale-up", and people end up "surrounded by digital activity and possibly even high nominal wealth, but structurally bankrupt and starved of functioning infrastructure, verifiable trust, and real utility". The 2021 Texas grid failure is cited as a precursor.

## Reliable safety net
Not addressed.

## Responsive government
Broken. The end-state is "a complete hollowing-out of human control", with the risk of "a succession event, ceding stewardship of a civilization we no longer understand". A geopolitical race pushes alignment to the minimum, and a few firms may come to "steer and tax the entire agentic economy". Elections are not discussed.

## Time for what matters
Not addressed. "Decaying human agency" is the scenario's theme, but the paper says nothing about time for family, community or meaning.

## Bottom line
A boom that is real on paper and hollow in practice: gains go to capital and a few super-experts, career ladders vanish, and human control erodes until the hidden debt comes due.
