# AI 2027: Slowdown ending — summary

*Kokotajlo, Alexander, Larsen, Lifland & Dean (AI Futures Project), April 2025 · the "slowdown" branch; the race branch is [`ai-2027`](../ai-2027/summary.md)*

**The scenario.** At the October 2027 fork, after a whistleblower reveals that OpenBrain's Agent-4 may be misaligned, a joint government–company **Oversight Committee** votes to slow down. It centralises compute, builds transparent "Safer" models and agrees a deal with China. By 2028–30 robots are commonplace, fusion and cures for many diseases arrive, and a bloodless, AI-assisted coup in China leads to a world government "under United Nations branding but obvious U.S. control." The authors say this ending "makes optimistic technical alignment assumptions" and is not a recommendation.

## Hard work pays
Broken. "In a few years, almost everything will be done by AIs and robots." "Some people work makeshift government jobs; others collect a generous basic income."

## Affordable essentials
Improving. "Even in developing countries, poverty becomes a thing of the past, thanks to UBI and foreign aid." But "billionaires become trillionaires" and scarce goods (Manhattan penthouses) move "even further out of the average person's reach."

## Reliable safety net
Improving. "Both parties promise a basic income for anyone who loses their job," and AI advisers make politicians generous "towards the economically dispossessed."

## Responsive government
Fragile. Like "an impoverished country sitting atop giant oil fields," government revenue comes from AI companies, a superintelligent surveillance system exists, and the Committee holds hard power. It allows a mostly fair 2028 election but must eventually either give control back to democratic institutions or entrench itself.

## Time for what matters
Hollowed out. "A society of superconsumers, spending our lives in an opium haze of amazing AI-provided luxuries and entertainment."

## Bottom line
Humanity survives and material promises are kept through UBI, while work disappears and democratic control rests on a committee's restraint.
