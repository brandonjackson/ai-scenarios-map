# AI 2027: Slowdown ending — summary

*Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland and Romeo Dean (AI Futures Project), "AI 2027", 3 April 2025. The more hopeful of two endings that share a path to October 2027; the Race ending is [its own scenario](../ai-2027/summary.md). The authors say it makes optimistic technical alignment assumptions, treat both a democratic and an oligarchic outcome as plausible, and do not endorse its policies. Read from the official PDF and branch page; see `extract.md`.*

**The scenario.** The shared story runs as in the Race ending: agents speed up AI research, junior software hiring weakens in 2026, and cheap agents spread across remote work in 2027. When evidence that Agent-4 may be misaligned reaches the government, a joint government–company committee pauses it, consolidates frontier oversight in the US and builds a more transparent "Safer" line. Superhuman assistants reach the public in 2028, a US–China treaty moves both sides to a jointly monitored successor, and factories in special economic zones drive very high growth. By 2029–30 robots, fusion and new medicines are widespread, poverty has ended, and who governs remains open.

## Hard work pays
Broken. Supervisors of AI gain early while entry-level programming contracts. Broad displacement arrives in 2028 and almost all tasks are expected to pass to AI and robots within a few years; even most remaining jobs become unnecessary. Some displaced people work in government-created roles, others live on basic income.

## Affordable essentials
Improving. New cures, cleaner and safer cities, energy innovations and income support raise living standards, and poverty ends even in poorer countries thanks to basic income and foreign aid. Positional goods stay scarce: everyone has "enough", but Manhattan penthouses move further out of reach as top fortunes grow.

## Reliable safety net
Improving. The White House first backs job training and unemployment insurance. In 2028 "Both parties promise a basic income for anyone who loses their job," and a generous income floor and international aid become the main ways output is shared. Revenue rises with growth, and later nearly all of it is expected to come from taxing, perhaps nationalising, AI firms.

## Responsive government
Fragile. The committee controls the most powerful systems and allows a mostly fair 2028 election. The story weighs two paths: gradually sharing control with Congress and the public, or entrenching an oligarchy. The state acquires powerful surveillance, and abroad democratic transitions lead to a strongly federal world government under UN branding with dominant US influence. The authors liken the revenue base to a resource-dependent state's.

## Time for what matters
Open. With almost all work automated, many embrace high-quality entertainment and consumption, others turn to religion or anti-consumerist lives, and 10% of Americans already counted an AI a close friend in 2027. The government leaves people to choose.

## Bottom line
Humanity survives and the material promises are kept through basic income and aid, while work disappears and democratic control rests on a small committee's restraint.
