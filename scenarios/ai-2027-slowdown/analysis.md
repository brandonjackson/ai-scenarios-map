# AI 2027: Slowdown ending — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**On: improvised and centralising.** Government intervention changes the course of frontier development and redistributes a share of the gains. In the scenario: retraining and unemployment insurance at first; a joint committee that pauses scaling, centralises frontier controls and builds safer models; special economic zones for industry; a jointly monitored US–China treaty; and finally a basic income, makeshift government jobs and foreign aid. It is crisis management rather than a planned programme, and the authors do not endorse it. The Race branch is [`ai-2027`](../ai-2027/analysis.md).

## Big assumptions
1. **Capability.** The same fast path as the Race ending, plus alignment that can be fixed in time: the Safer line is more transparent and controllable. The authors call this optimistic.
2. **Adoption.** Public release makes superhuman assistants widespread in 2028; factories in special zones automate physical work; regulators and agencies use the systems to move faster.
3. **Markets.** Very high growth by October 2028, with tax receipts rising alongside; holders of the right AI investments gain disproportionately.
4. **Politics.** Evidence of misalignment is believed, the committee votes to slow down, China agrees a treaty without a military victory, and committee members allow a fair election.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Misalignment evidence emerges early enough and is acted on despite the race with China.
- Frontier development can be consolidated under US oversight without losing the lead.
- A verifiable treaty is possible between rivals.
- Revenue from AI firms grows fast enough to fund a generous income floor and foreign aid.

## Tensions and weak points
- The good ending relies on a rentier state: the authors compare nearly all revenue coming from AI firms to resource-dependent states, the dynamic *The Intelligence Curse* warns about.
- Who governs is left open; the democratic path depends on the restraint of the people who hold the systems.
- **Map.** The fiscal point (−0.70, 0.60) stays: few good jobs, strong finances. On gains it moves from −0.55 to −0.40 and now sits less concentrated than the Race ending (−0.60): the new extract stresses poverty ending in poorer countries through aid alongside rising inequality, while in Race control ends with one system. Labour neighbours are `korinek-agi`, `sit-awareness` and `tcb-displacement`; on the fiscal plot its nearest neighbour is now `ai-2040-plan-a`, its authors' later and more deliberate version of the same idea.
- No growth rate, budget, debt or basic-income level is given.

## Scoring notes
Scores are 1/4/4/2/3, all stated. Work is 1: almost all tasks pass to AI and robots and the remaining jobs are makeshift. Essentials is 4: poverty ends even in poorer countries and medicine and energy improve, held below 5 because scarce housing moves further out of reach. Safety net is 4: a generous income floor and aid that both parties back. Government is 2: a small committee controls the most powerful systems and surveillance, and may or may not hand power back. Time is 3: the extract describes a range of choices (consumption, religion, anti-consumerism) that government leaves to people, neither a gain nor a harm. The weakest-link rule caps the headline at 2, against 1 for the Race ending.
