# AI 2027: Slowdown ending — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Policy on, improvised and centralising.** The government takes joint control of the leading lab, consolidates compute, negotiates with China and introduces a basic income. Policy here is crisis management, not planned reform. The race branch is [`ai-2027`](../ai-2027/analysis.md).

## Big assumptions
1. **Alignment can be fixed in time.** Faithful chain-of-thought and transparent "Safer" models work. The authors call this optimistic.
2. **A whistleblower and public backlash force a pause,** and the Committee votes the right way.
3. **The US lead survives a slowdown** thanks to compute consolidation.
4. **Committee members act virtuously,** allowing fair elections and, perhaps, giving up power.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Misalignment evidence emerges early enough and is believed.
- Compute can be centralised (for example, through the Defense Production Act) without collapse in public trust.
- AI-generated tax revenue funds a generous basic income.

## Tensions and weak points
- The good ending relies on the rentier-state dynamics *The Intelligence Curse* warns about.
- "Who rules the future?" is left open: the Committee could entrench itself.

## Scoring notes
1/4/4/2/2, average 2.6. The weakest-link rule caps it at 2, against 1 for the race branch.
