# Runaway Diffusion — summary

*IMF Note 2026/002, "Global Economic and Financial Implications of Artificial Intelligence: Lessons from a Scenario Planning Exercise" (3 April 2026). It synthesises a December 2025 workshop the IMF ran with the Economics of Transformative AI Initiative (University of Virginia). Both scenarios assume AI can perform a wide range of cognitive and manual tasks by the early 2030s; they differ in how fast and how freely it diffuses. A companion PIIE blog by Dabla-Norris and Korinek (February 2026) describes the same pair. Authors: Barhoumi, de Carvalho, Gorbanyov, Kido, Koll, Nguyen, Ostojic, Shang, Tamirisa (lead), Toms and Zhao, under the guidance of Dabla-Norris. Read from the note's full text; see `extract.md`.*

**The scenario.** "AI diffuses rapidly across sectors under minimal regulatory constraints, propelled in part by intelligent AI agents." Firms chase cost savings, AI is built into robots that automate manufacturing, construction, mining, logistics and transport, and in much of the economy "humans become optional rather than necessary". Output and productivity rise faster than in the baseline.

## Hard work pays
Broken for many. Displacement is broad and front-loaded: task automation and AI-powered robots "displace routine cognitive and physical jobs in both manufacturing and service sectors". Some high-skilled workers in AI-complementary jobs "benefit disproportionately", but "capital owners capture a large share of the gains". Extensive automation "reduces the economic role of migrants and strengthens political incentives to restrict immigration".

## Affordable essentials
Ambiguous. Automation expands supply, but displaced households weaken demand; the note does not say which wins.

## Reliable safety net
Under severe strain. The note warns of eroding labour tax bases and rising social-spending needs, especially where social insurance is tied to employment. Job losses weaken household balance sheets and raise default risk for banks exposed to consumer credit.

## Responsive government
Weakened by concentration. A small number of large firms dominate AI through better models, data and compute, and gains pool in metropolitan hubs, widening regional gaps. Capital flows to jurisdictions with favourable tax, energy and data access. Regulation is minimal by assumption.

## Time for what matters
Not addressed.

## Bottom line
Fast growth with the gains going to capital and a few firms and places, while the tax base that pays for the safety net shrinks.
