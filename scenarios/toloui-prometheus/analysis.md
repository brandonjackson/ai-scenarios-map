# Promethean Fire — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Policy on.** This is Toloui's target, and his five priorities are designed to reach it: labour-favouring tax reform, public R&D and procurement for worker-augmenting AI, modern antitrust, institutionalised safety, and energy and chip infrastructure. Historically, he credits public education and public infrastructure with making the original Promethean Fire possible. It isn't the market default.

## Big assumptions
1. **Intelligence becomes a commodity.** Brute-force scaling stops paying, efficiency improves, small specialised models suffice, and several giants keep competing. Value moves to applications, as with electricity.
2. **AI stays complementary at scale.** Even transformative AI leaves important human attributes in demand, and cheaper output raises labour demand by more than it saves.
3. **Diffusion is fast but absorbable.** Organisations reorganise workflows and build skills quickly enough to capture augmentation gains.
4. **The state can contain misuse.** Decentralised capability brings cyber, bio and disinformation risks. The scenario assumes these are managed with transparency, evaluations and liability, without a crackdown that re-centralises the field.
5. **Energy and chips scale** without passing costs to households.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Open and fast-follower models stay close to the frontier, and leaderboards stay "competitive and highly fluid".
- Firms pursue augmentation even though automation is often the quicker way to show returns.
- Workers and small firms get access, skills and data rights (portability, interoperability, regulatory sandboxes, extension programmes).
- Misuse and misalignment don't produce a disaster that kills public support. Toloui notes that preventing AI accidents is "essential for sustaining societal support for AI diffusion".
- US–China racing doesn't turn AI into a securitised, concentrated national project.

## Tensions and weak points
- The essay is candid that "the jury is out". Promethean Fire is the best case, chosen as a policy target, not forecast.
- The historical analogy may not transfer. Electricity needed humans to operate the machines; transformative AI is meant to "surpass human intelligence across multiple fields".
- Decentralisation and safety pull against each other. CFG's "Decentralised Mayhem" ending shows how open diffusion can end in harmonised bans.
