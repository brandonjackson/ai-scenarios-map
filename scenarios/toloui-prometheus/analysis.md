# Promethean Fire — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Mixed.** The quadrant is defined by market conditions (competitive diffusion and augmentation), which can arise without new policy. But the positive form is Toloui's explicit policy goal, and in his historical analogues public education and infrastructure helped deliver it. The extract names no fixed AI policy package for the quadrant; the five priorities (labour-favouring tax reform, worker-augmenting R&D and procurement, competition rules, safety governance, energy and chips) are recommendations across the set, so they are not counted as assumed. Previously rated `on`; the new extract's stance supports `mixed`.

## Big assumptions
1. **Capability commoditises.** Efficiency improvements, investment by several large firms, open models near the frontier and good-enough specialised models keep catch-up feasible and switching costs low.
2. **Adoption favours augmentation.** Firms do the harder work of redesigning workflows around workers, although the extract notes automating may be easier at first.
3. **Markets pass gains through.** Falling unit costs reach consumers and raise demand for labour along supply chains and in new industries.
4. **Politics invests.** Government supplies the education, compute access, extension services and safety governance that widen who can use the technology, and contains misuse that could erode public support.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Open and fast-follower models stay close enough to the frontier that the edge moves to applications.
- Complementary human attributes (empathy, presence, judgement, imagination, leadership) stay in demand.
- Workers and small firms gain the skills and access to deploy AI.
- No major misuse or misalignment incident turns the public against diffusion.
- US–China rivalry does not push towards concentration or reckless speed.

## Tensions and weak points
- The case rests on historical analogues (autos, appliances, sanitation), not on a projection for AI. The essay gives no AI growth path or figures for this quadrant.
- Decentralised capability spreads misuse risk too, which the extract flags across the set.
- The map treats this as the most diffuse Toloui quadrant (0.58, -0.55); the extract supports this but says complementarities vary by field, skill and task, so diffuse gains are not guaranteed for everyone.
- Close neighbours: `turing-transform`, `turing-trap-escape`, `cfg-plateau` and `korinek-aug`.

## Scoring notes
Draft scores 4/4*/3*/–/– (previously 4/4/3/3/3). Work is 4 because augmentation, cheaper output and new industries raise labour demand, with early evidence favouring less experienced workers. Essentials is 4, inferred: competition cuts the unit cost of AI and the extract's historical mechanism passes lower costs on to final prices. Safety net is 3, inferred: the larger supports are reserved for strong displacement, which this augmenting quadrant avoids. Government and time are not addressed: the extract is silent on political voice, and the only time evidence is the historical appliance analogue. On the fiscal map, public finances (`y_fiscal` 0.5) are inferred from rising labour demand under a tax system the extract says leans on income and payroll taxes; the essay gives no fiscal path.
