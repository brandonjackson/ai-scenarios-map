# Slow Burn — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Policy off by design.** GO-Science says the scenarios "do not account for any policy intervention from the UK government" so they can be used to test policy. Populist opposition and cultural resistance, not regulation, are what hold adoption back.

## Big assumptions
1. **Progress stalls after 2028** because of compute, architecture and data limits, not because anyone chooses to slow it.
2. **Capability is uneven.** AI matches people on scoped digital work and writes nearly all code, yet still fails at open-ended work. Both have to hold at once.
3. **A bubble deflates without a crash.** The 2028 correction hits credit and start-ups but does not cause a recession.
4. **Displacement stays at the bottom of the ladder.** Losses fall on junior and execution roles, or show up only as slower hiring.
5. **Distribution is moderately concentrated.** The US keeps a narrow lead, while open and closed models converge and China gains through deployment and industrial capacity.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Scaling and algorithmic gains run out within about two years.
- Firms keep humans checking agents' work rather than delegating fully.
- Public opposition is strong enough to deter adoption in health, education and criminal justice.
- The 2028 correction stays contained to AI firms and start-ups.

## Tensions and weak points
- Today's trends cut both ways. Code-writing capability is close to the scenario's 2030 level already, but investment and capability are still climbing in late 2026, not slowing.
- "Contained to junior roles" is still a lost-generation problem (compare `wf-lost-generation`). The scenario treats it as modest because the headline numbers are small.
- The most serious harms are to trust and young people's relationships, not to jobs. That makes it unusual among low-disruption scenarios.
- GO-Science notes a 2040 risk: if adoption bottlenecks clear or a new architecture arrives, displacement could speed up sharply.

## Scoring notes
Draft scores are 3/3/3/2/2. Work at 3 is a judgement call: the young bear real costs, which could justify a 2, but the source stresses how limited displacement is. Government and time get 2 because the source names specific, serious harms to democratic trust and young people's relationships.
