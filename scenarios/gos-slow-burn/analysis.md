# Slow Burn — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Off.** New government interventions are outside the scenario's scope. What holds adoption back is reliability, liability, public opposition and organisational limits; trade controls and existing legal constraints are background, not choices the scenario depends on.

## Big assumptions
1. **Capability stalls after 2028.** Compute scarcity, architecture limits and a shortage of good data slow progress, while AI still matches experts on bounded digital work.
2. **Adoption stays shallow.** Use is widespread but agents get little access or autonomy, and gains concentrate in well-resourced digital-native firms.
3. **A correction without a crash.** The 2028 market correction cuts credit and start-ups but causes no recession, and markets recover by 2030.
4. **Politics pushes back.** Populist opposition and public resistance deter AI in sensitive sectors.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Scaling and algorithmic gains run out within about two years of today.
- Firms keep people supervising agents rather than delegating fully.
- The correction stays inside AI firms and start-ups.
- Price competition keeps AI cheap while open and closed models converge.

## Tensions and weak points
- In late 2026 investment and capability are still climbing, so the stall is the scenario's least certain step; code-writing is already near its 2030 level.
- "Contained" displacement still lands on junior workers, who take significant pay cuts. It is a small-numbers version of `wf-lost-generation`.
- The previous map entry tagged it concentrated while placing it on the diffuse side. Large-cap and digital-native firms gain most, so it now sits just left of centre (x −0.1), close to `imf-baseline` and `anthropic-modest`.
- Household incomes, government finances and the safety net are not addressed, so the fiscal placement (y −0.05) rests only on the minimal growth uplift and the temporary drag.
- Its sharpest harms are to democratic trust and young people's relationships, unusual for a low-disruption scenario.
