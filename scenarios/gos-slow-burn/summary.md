# Slow Burn — summary

*UK Government Office for Science, with AISI and DSIT, "AI Scenarios 2030: Helping policymakers plan for the future of AI" (15 June 2026)*

**The scenario.** "AI progress slows, and AI causes less disruption than expected." After 2028, compute limits, the limits of current architectures and scarce high-quality data slow progress. By 2030 AI automates digital workflows "as capably as humans" and writes nearly all code, but still struggles with novel insight, open-ended tasks and learning on the job, and needs intermittent human help. An AI market correction in 2028 shrinks credit and investment and many start-ups collapse, a "temporary drag on UK growth but did not cause a recession."

## Hard work pays
Mostly holds. Displacement is "contained to the most junior and execution-oriented roles or only leads to hiring reductions", mainly in software engineering, customer service and accountancy. Those displaced "often accept significant wage reductions", and few new roles appear beyond integrating and auditing AI. In most sectors, such as health and education, little changes. Work shifts towards managing agents, framing problems and checking results.

## Affordable essentials
Unchanged. "AI creates minimal economic uplift", so nothing makes housing, food, energy or care cheaper or dearer.

## Reliable safety net
Not tested. The correction is short and shallow and displacement is contained, so existing support copes.

## Responsive government
Strained by the information environment. "Synthetic media routinely creates false narratives about major events", and hostile states deploy persuasive agents at scale ahead of elections, "eroding trust in the information environment and democratic processes." Public opposition is considerable, with populist parties campaigning against AI. Strategic competition with China continues, but humans stay "in the loop" in military uses.

## Time for what matters
Worse for the young. "Many young people have come to depend on AI companions for friendship, therapy, and romance, reducing their resilience and motivation to form real human relationships."

## Bottom line
The calmest of the five futures for jobs and incomes, but not a calm one for democracy or young people's lives. GO-Science warns that slow progress could still be overturned by sudden breakthroughs from new architectures.
