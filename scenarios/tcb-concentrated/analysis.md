# Concentrated Gains — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Off.** The scenario describes what happens without targeted help; the report's cross-cutting recommendations are listed under `tcb-augmentation`.

## Big assumptions
1. **Gains follow existing advantage.** Firms and metros with AI skills and capital capture most of the productivity growth.
2. **Limited net job loss.** The harm is relative, not mass unemployment.
3. **Exposure varies by occupation.** Office and STEM work is more exposed than manual trades.

## What must be true for this to occur
- AI productivity gains are real but accrue to adopters rather than spreading through prices or wages.
- Workers can't easily move to AI hubs or into AI-using roles.

## Tensions and weak points
- Much of it is already visible (metro concentration of AI hiring, customer-service losses), which is why it is rated Probable.
- Close to `wef-stalled` and `acemoglu` on modest, concentrated gains.
- The "AI underclass" is asserted rather than modelled.
