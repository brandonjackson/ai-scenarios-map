# Uneven Disruption — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Off.** The targeted-assistance ideas are suggestions, not part of the scenario.

## Big assumptions
1. **Creation offsets destruction** in aggregate, as with past technologies.
2. **Displacement is concentrated** by occupation and place.
3. **Adjustment is slow and costly** for those displaced.

## What must be true for this to occur
- AI creates enough new roles to absorb most displaced workers over time.
- Labour markets reallocate, but slowly and with lasting earnings losses.

## Tensions and weak points
- It is the scenario most consistent with economic history, which is why it is rated Probable.
- Thin on specifics: the report names "certain sectors and roles" without saying which.
- Close to `ilo` and `acemoglu`, and to the trade-shock literature.

## Scoring notes
Draft scores are 2/3/2/3/3. Work and safety net are 2 because the source documents large, lasting losses for a specific group and benefit systems built for statewide, not occupational, shocks. The rest are not addressed.
