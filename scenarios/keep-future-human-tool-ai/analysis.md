# Keep the Future Human: Tool AI — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**Strongly policy on.** It needs compute oversight, hardware-enforced caps, strict liability, tiered regulation and international agreements. The policy-off twin is [`keep-future-human`](../keep-future-human/analysis.md).

## Big assumptions
1. **Compute is governable** through the small number of firms that make AI chips.
2. **Tool AI delivers most of the benefit** without autonomous general agents.
3. **The Tool/AGI line can be drawn and held** in practice.
4. **Political will arrives in time,** which Aguirre expects to come from "reality itself".

## What must be true for this to occur
- The US, China and others agree on verifiable compute limits.
- Tool AI is commercially attractive enough to win investment without the AGI prize.
- Liability safe harbours successfully steer developers towards limited, controllable systems.

## Tensions and weak points
- Agents assembled from "tools" may become autonomous anyway.
- Narayanan & Kapoor and Buterin warn that non-proliferation and licensing concentrate power and can be fragile.
