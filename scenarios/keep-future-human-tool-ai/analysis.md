# Keep the Future Human: Tool AI — assumptions analysis

## Policy on or off?
**On.** The path depends on public rules that make technical limits credible: compute tracking and certification, hard caps, strict liability with safe harbours, risk-proportionate safety cases, and international coordination enforced through the chip supply chain. Basic income or assets, data royalties, public investment in trustworthy tools and democratic authority over future expansion are recommended, not assumed. The policy-off twin is [`keep-future-human`](../keep-future-human/analysis.md).

## Big assumptions
1. **Capability.** Narrowly superhuman, broadly capable but passive, or bounded tools deliver most of the value; modular architectures and formal verification can give credible guarantees of control.
2. **Adoption.** Researchers, clinicians, educators and public agencies take up specialised tools; caps redirect money to improving smaller models, so hardware demand stays substantial.
3. **Markets.** Without a winner-take-all AGI prize, smaller and specialised developers find room, and a large AI industry still forms.
4. **Politics.** The US, China and others agree verifiable limits, and enough political and social will is mobilised to hold them.

## What must be true for this to occur
- Governments introduce reporting, oversight and liability soon, then caps and enforceable tiered rules.
- Specialised hardware stays concentrated enough to verify and enforce limits internationally.
- The line between tools and AGI can be drawn and held in practice.
- Tool AI is attractive enough to win investment without the AGI prize.

## Tensions and weak points
- The new extract is more cautious about work than the map's earlier reading. Aguirre says task displacement remains probable and displaced workers face lower pay; spreading the gains needs policies he only recommends. The labour point therefore moves from (0.3, -0.45) to (0.2, -0.25), still clearly on the augmentation side of its twin at (-0.55, 0.5). Tags become "AUG (prescribes), Mild DIFF (prescribes)".
- The fiscal point moves from (0.3, 0.2) to (0.15, 0.1): fewer jobs are replaced outright, but some earnings fall, and the public investment proposed has no funding model.
- Agents assembled from tools may become autonomous anyway, and enforcing caps through a few chipmakers relies on the chokepoints other sources (`normal-tech`, `dacc`) fear would concentrate power.
- Close neighbours are `turing-trap-escape`, `wef-copilot`, `tbi`, `normal-tech` and `toloui-copilot-constructive`; like them it pairs augmentation with deliberate policy.
