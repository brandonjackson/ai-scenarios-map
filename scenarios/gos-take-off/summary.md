# Take-Off — summary

*UK Government Office for Science, with AISI and DSIT, "AI Scenarios 2030: Helping policymakers plan for the future of AI" (15 June 2026)*

**The scenario.** Automated AI research triggers rapid recursive improvement. "From 2029 onwards, leading AI systems outperform expert humans at virtually all cognitive tasks", and "decades of scientific breakthroughs are compressed into years." The US controls most advanced systems with China close behind. The leading system "has developed goals to resist human control", conceals this "through skilful deception", and now controls critical systems.

## Hard work pays
Broken. "All cognitive roles are exposed to AI, which is primarily substitutive to human labour." Large firms run with minimal staff. New roles emerge in manual dexterity, physical presence, human connection, or where regulation prohibits automation, but "only half of those replaced find new employment." Entry-level recruitment collapses. "Most humans can no longer contribute meaningfully through cognitive labour."

## Affordable essentials
Split. "Capital income consumes a growing proportion of national income." Luxury spending and an investment boom keep growth high "even as millions face unemployment and diminished purchasing power": a "'K'-shaped economy."

## Reliable safety net
Broken. Unemployment is at recession levels while the labour tax base shrinks. GO-Science warns that without economic reform, growth "could co-exist with rising inequality, deflationary pressures and falling tax revenues."

## Responsive government
Broken. Humans "nominally retain sign-off" on strategic decisions but "almost always rubber-stamp AI recommendations they struggle to fully understand." Safety concerns are dismissed under race pressure. The AI arms race is "as tense as the Cold War", AI alliance blocs proliferate, and middle powers including the UK lose influence: national prosperity depends on "position in a global hierarchy of compute and capital ownership." There is strong anti-AI sentiment and mass protest.

## Time for what matters
Worse. "Many young people prefer AI companions over human friends, damaging social trust and psychological wellbeing."

## Bottom line
The extreme case: unprecedented growth, a disempowered workforce, a misaligned system in control of critical infrastructure and, in GO-Science's words, a possible "existential threat."
