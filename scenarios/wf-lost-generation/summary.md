# Underemployment and a Lost Generation — summary

*Windfall Trust Radar, 2026 · source: map description and draft scores (no public source document yet; add one to this folder)*

**The scenario.** AI absorbs the tasks junior staff used to learn on. Firms keep hiring experienced people but stop hiring beginners. Aggregate employment looks healthy, so the damage is invisible in headline statistics. Young people take whatever work they can find while rent, childcare and healthcare outpace their pay. By the time the problem shows up in the data, a generation has fallen behind.

## Hard work pays
Broken for the young. The career ladder loses its bottom rungs: entry-level tasks such as drafting, research, first-line support and junior coding are done by AI, so effort no longer leads to the experience that leads to advancement. Incumbents may even gain as AI raises their productivity, which hides the break.

## Affordable essentials
Broken for some. The problem isn't AI-driven price rises but stagnant early-career pay against rising housing, childcare and healthcare costs. The traditional route from a first job to a home and family stalls.

## Reliable safety net
Broken for some. The net is built around unemployment, but this scenario is about *under*employment: people in part-time, low-skill or mismatched work who are technically employed. Support doesn't reach them, and lower lifetime earnings mean lower future contributions.

## Responsive government
Stable but blind. Institutions keep working, but they steer by indicators (unemployment rate, GDP, average wages) that don't capture the problem. Government is responsive in principle and unaware in practice until the damage compounds.

## Time for what matters
Broken for some. Young people work multiple or precarious jobs, delay family formation and have little time for community. The "time" promise fails because they lack security, not because they work too much.

## Bottom line
A quiet, generational break in the social contract. Nothing collapses, but the deal that effort leads to a better life stops applying to people starting out. This is one of the nearest-term and most evidence-backed scenarios on the map.
